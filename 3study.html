Chapter 7 · Plant water relations

Water potential and plant response to water deficit

Water movement through plant tissues is governed by differences in water potential. A plant exposed to declining soil water availability must maintain water uptake while limiting unnecessary water loss.

As water deficit develops, stomatal conductance can decline, reducing transpiration and altering photosynthetic gas exchange. The response is not simply a definition to memorize: the timing, severity, and physiological consequences depend on the interacting mechanisms.

Study prompt: What mechanism connects declining water availability to the observed stomatal response?

Consider the experiment. Identify the treatment, response variable, and expected pattern before reading the reported result. Then explain what the graph would mean physiologically.

A strong explanation moves from observation → graph → interpretation → mechanism → conclusion. If the evidence does not support a causal claim, say so and identify what additional experiment would be needed.
