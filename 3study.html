BEFORE YOU HIGHLIGHT✦ Highlight Guide

Ask what this passage is doing before choosing a mark.

Key ideaImportant concept, principle, definition, or statement.Ask: Is this something I need to understand and remember?
MechanismExplains how or why something happens.Ask: Does this explain a process, cause, pathway, mechanism, or sequence?
ConnectionRelates this idea to another concept, topic, experiment, or course.Ask: Can this be relevant somewhere else I am studying?
EvidenceExperiment, observation, result, figure, data, or example supporting a claim.Ask: Is this evidence showing that the idea is supported?
GapSomething unclear, incomplete, conflicting, or worth revisiting.Ask: Do I understand this well enough to explain it without looking?
UnderlineMark something worth returning to without assigning a category.Ask: Do I want to find this passage again later?
